Every object in the arcade is built from boxes, cylinders and planes. The room is lit by one warm
hemisphere light and a single directional "sun" with soft shadows, which keeps the scene cheap and the
colors readable.

```tsx
<mesh castShadow>
  <boxGeometry args={[1, 2, 0.8]} />
  <meshStandardMaterial color="#e45e4d" roughness={0.45} />
</mesh>
```

Labels are drawn on a 2D canvas and uploaded as a texture, so Persian text shapes correctly for free.
