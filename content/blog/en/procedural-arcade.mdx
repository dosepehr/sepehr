Every object in the arcade is built from boxes, cylinders and planes. The neon look comes from
emissive materials and a bloom pass rather than real lights, which keeps the scene cheap.

```tsx
<mesh>
  <boxGeometry args={[1, 2, 0.8]} />
  <meshStandardMaterial emissive="#ff2d95" emissiveIntensity={2} toneMapped={false} />
</mesh>
```

Labels are drawn on a 2D canvas and uploaded as a texture, so Persian text shapes correctly for free.
