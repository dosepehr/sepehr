## The idea

A portfolio people remember: a neon arcade where each cabinet is a project, the CRT desk is a terminal,
and there are games and secrets to find.

## How it's built

- **100% procedural 3D.** No model files. Cabinets, desk and props are primitives, labels are drawn on a canvas.
- **One content source, two views.** The same MDX feeds the 3D room and the classic 2D pages, so SEO and accessibility don't depend on WebGL.
- **Lazy 3D.** The three.js bundle only loads on capable desktop devices. Phones get a fast 2D "Lite" hub.
